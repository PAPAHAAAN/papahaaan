BEFORE OUR FIRST HELLO

A few things
you might be wondering.

What is Haji?

Haji is Hajiloop’s AI companion for understanding your everyday wellbeing. It helps you talk through sleep, recovery, movement, and how you feel, with a focus on one manageable next step.

Haji supports reflection and everyday choices. It doesn’t diagnose conditions or replace medical care.

What information does Hajiloop use?

Available sleep, recovery, and movement records, plus how you feel and the everyday context you choose to share. An optional Apple Health connection is planned for the iPhone app.

Joining this waitlist does not connect to your health data.

Which devices will it support?

Hajiloop is being built for iPhone. Supported models, iOS versions, and whether Hajiloop One is required at launch have not been announced.

What happens after I join?

After you join, we’ll save your place and send a confirmation email. Launch updates will go to the same address. You only need to sign up once.

When will early access begin?

A launch date hasn’t been announced yet. We’ll share timing by email.

How does the one-year offer work?

Waitlist members get their first year of Hajiloop free at launch. The exact access terms and pricing after the free period haven’t been announced.

How is my waitlist information handled?

We store your email, signup time, and how you found Hajiloop to manage the waitlist and send launch updates. Email inputs are masked in Clarity session replays, and we don’t send your email in analytics events.

Read our Privacy information. For questions or removal requests, email loopnote.hq@gmail.com.
